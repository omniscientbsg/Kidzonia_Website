It’s ten past four and the meeting is overrunning.

Every working parent knows this specific feeling.

It isn’t really about logistics — you can solve logistics. It’s the quiet worry that the hours between school ending and you arriving are hours your child is simply being contained somewhere. That’s the thing we built this to fix.

Kidzonia daycare isn’t a room where children wait. It’s a properly planned afternoon in the same building your child already knows, staffed by the same adults, following the same safety rules and the same thinking about how children learn. There are naps and real rest. There are quiet corners for a child who needs twenty minutes on her own. There’s someone who notices your son hasn’t eaten his lunch two days running, and who tells you.

You’ll get a proper handover at pick-up, whatever time you arrive. Not “he was fine.” What he did, what he ate, what was hard.

Four kinds of family, one daycare.

6 to 18 months

Infants

Care built around your baby’s routine rather than ours — their feed times, their nap times, their comfort object. Handled by consistent adults, so it’s the same face every day.

18 months to 3 years

Toddlers

Full-day care with the structure a toddler needs and the flexibility a toddler demands. Can run alongside Playgroup.

3 to 6 years

Preschoolers

The natural extension of the school day. Morning programme, lunch, rest, and a genuinely good afternoon.

6 to 12 years

After-school

For older children between school ending and you finishing. Space to do homework, space to do nothing, and adults who know the difference between the two.
