Corporate Daycare · For employers

The reason your best people leave is often a childcare problem.

Kidzonia partners with employers across Navi Mumbai, Pune and Hyderabad to solve it properly — through on-site facilities or reserved places at centres near your office.

Talk to our corporate teamThree ways to partner
3ways to partner 45+centres for your teams

It’s a retention problem wearing a childcare costume.

The exit interview says “personal reasons.”

The attrition pattern is familiar to any HR team in an Indian metro: a valued employee returns from parental leave, manages for a few months, and then leaves. The actual reason is usually that the childcare arrangement was fragile and something broke.

Employer-supported childcare addresses that directly — and, for many organisations, addresses a statutory obligation at the same time.

Three ways to partner.

From a facility inside your building to a negotiated rate at ours.

01 · Most involved

On-site crèche

We design, staff and run a facility inside your premises. You provide the space; we handle everything else — curriculum, staffing, safety compliance, daily operations.

02 · Fastest to set up

Near-site places

Reserved capacity for your employees at Kidzonia centres close to your offices. Faster to set up, no space required, and it works across multiple locations.

03 · Lightest touch

Corporate rates

Your employees enrol directly at any Kidzonia centre at negotiated rates, with priority admission.

What your employees get

  • Preschool and daycare, 8:00am to 8:00pm, ages 6 months to 12 years
  • The same DISCOVER curriculum and safety standards as every Kidzonia centre
  • Access at 45+ centres — useful for hybrid teams and staff spread across a city
  • Holiday and vacation cover

What you get

  • A visible, tangible benefit that employees actually use
  • A measurable lever on post-parental-leave retention
  • Support with statutory crèche compliance
  • A single operating partner across multiple cities

How it works.

Five steps, from first conversation to a facility we run for you.

01Conversation

Your locations, headcount and what you’re trying to solve.

02Proposal

The model that fits, with costs and a timeline.

03Design

Space planning, staffing and compliance.

04Launch

Doors open for your employees’ children.

05Run

We operate it; you get regular reporting.
