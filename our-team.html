The Teachers

And the several hundred people who actually do the work.

Teachers and assistants across our centres. They’re the reason any of this is real, and they’re who you should be assessing on your visit — not the reception area.

Every adult in the building is background-verified before their first day. After that, the best test is simply to watch them for twenty minutes.

  • Do they know every child’s name, and use it?
  • Do they get down to a child’s eye level, or talk from above?
  • When two children argue, do they solve it for them or help them solve it?
  • What do they do with the child who doesn’t want to join in?
