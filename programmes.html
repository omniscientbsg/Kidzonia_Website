Our Programmes · 2 to 6 years, and daycare to 12

Four rooms. Four completely different humans.

The gap between a two-year-old and a five-year-old is enormous — bigger, developmentally, than the gap between a fifteen-year-old and an adult. So we don’t run one programme with four labels on it. We run four genuinely different programmes.

Not sure where your child fits?

Age is the starting point, not the whole answer.

A child who’s just turned three but has never been in a group before may do far better beginning in Playgroup. A confident four-year-old who’s already been at another preschool may be ready for Kidzo Junior straight away.

Tell us a little about your child and we’ll tell you honestly where we think they’d settle best — even if that’s a shorter programme than you were expecting.

After six

And when they outgrow us.

Kidzo Senior is designed to hand your child to their next school ready — not just academically, but socially, and with the stamina for a longer day. We’ll help with the transition, including school-readiness conversations in the final term.

Daycare, meanwhile, doesn’t stop at six. After-school care continues right up to 12.
