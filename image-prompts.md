# Kidzonia image prompts

Prompts for every photo slot on the six built pages (18 images). Each prompt is self-contained, so it can be pasted straight into an image generator.

## Look and feel, taken from kidzonia.in

- **Children:** Indian children aged 2 to 6, real and candid rather than posed, mid-activity, often glancing at the camera with a genuine smile.
- **Uniform:** a sunny yellow polo shirt with grey collar and sleeve trim, and grey track pants with a yellow side stripe. Some younger children wear a grey or green polo instead.
- **Rooms:** bright, clean preschool rooms with yellow and purple-blue walls, low white cubbies, small chairs in primary colours, and colourful toys such as building blocks, abacus, play fruit and puzzles.
- **Light and camera:** soft natural daylight, warm and airy. Eye level with the child, shallow depth of field, and a background that blurs into soft colour.
- **Palette:** the site's yellow `#F9C93C`, pink `#E93E7E`, green `#79BF43`, teal `#2BAFC8` and purple `#8E5BB5` should show up in toys, walls and props. Nothing should be neon.
- **Avoid:** text, logos or lettering anywhere in the image (generators garble them), stock-photo stiffness, Western-looking classrooms, adults' faces dominating the frame, and clutter or mess that reads as dirty.

**Before launch:** these images are fine for building the look and feel. For the live site, real photos from Kidzonia centres will earn more trust from parents. Use these prompts as a shot list for the photographer.

```
text, letters, logo, watermark, signage, distorted hands, extra fingers, deformed faces, uncanny eyes, plastic skin, oversaturated, neon, dark, gloomy, messy dirty room, Western classroom, stock photo pose, adult face in focus
```

## Full-width page heroes (6)

Every page opens with a full-width hero. Its left half sits under a dark purple gradient with the headline on top, so **keep the left 45% calm and low-detail and put the subject in the right half.** Generate at **2400×1200 (2:1)**. The page crops the image, so keep faces away from the top and bottom edges.
