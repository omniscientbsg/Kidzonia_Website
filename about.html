We started with one room and a fairly stubborn opinion.

That small children learn best when they’re allowed to move, touch, ask and get it wrong — and that a preschool ought to be built around that fact rather than around a timetable.

What hasn’t changed since that first room is the idea underneath it. Children learn best through play and exploration, and a preschool’s job is to give them a safe, nurturing and genuinely stimulating place to do exactly that.

Today the same idea runs across 45+ centres in Navi Mumbai, Pune, Hyderabad, Indore and Dewas, through six programmes from Playgroup to Corporate Daycare.

Read our philosophy

Four things we’re fairly stubborn about.

None of them are slogans. Each one changes what happens in a room on an ordinary morning.

01

Play is the method, not the reward.

It doesn’t come after the learning. It’s how the learning happens.

02

Every child arrives already clever.

The job isn’t to fill them up. It’s to find out what kind of clever they are and build from there.

03

Parents deserve reasons, not reassurance.

If we can’t explain why we do something, we probably shouldn’t be doing it.

04

Consistency is the point of scale.

Forty-five centres is only meaningful if the forty-fifth is as good as the first.

What we’re here to do.

The official version, in plain words. If you only read one card, read the middle one.

VisionEvery child reaching their full potential, mostly by playing.

We believe children learn best through play and exploration. So we build places that are safe, nurturing and a little noisy, where social, emotional, physical and cognitive development happen through hands-on exploration, creativity and self-expression. A thematic programme, with digital learning used on purpose, makes it something children experience rather than recite.

MissionThe whole child, not just the part that gets tested.

High-quality early childhood education that looks after academic, social, emotional and physical development together. A safe, stimulating environment that encourages curiosity, creativity and independence. And a real partnership with families, so what happens here carries on at home and into the next school.

ValuesA community children want to belong to.

Respect for diversity. Inclusivity as the default, not an initiative. A sense of community between children, parents and teachers. And a love of learning that, if we’ve done our job, lasts a great deal longer than their time with us.
